# Executive Demonstration Script — Resyntel
### Live Presentation Guide for Hotel CEOs, CFOs & General Managers

---

## Pitch Overview

- **Audience**: Hotel CEO, CFO, General Manager, or Engineering Director.
- **Duration**: 8–12 minutes.
- **Core Narrative**: *“Hotels already have the data in their meters and BMS. What they lack is an intelligence layer that translates kilowatt-hours and cubic meters into immediate Dirham savings.”*
- **Demo Property**: **Zephyr Marrakech** (180 keys, Marrakech, Morocco).

---

## 10-Step Presentation Walkthrough

### Step 1: The Landing Page (Positioning & Credibility)
- **URL**: [https://resyntel.vercel.app](https://resyntel.vercel.app)
- **Talking Points**:
  - Introduce **RESYNTEL** (*Resource Intelligence for Hospitality · An EM300.co Company*).
  - Highlight the core thesis: *“Know where your hotel is wasting resources. Know what it is costing you.”*
  - Demonstrate the floating intelligence instrument: **65,200 MAD** estimated annual avoidable cost and the active **5.4 m³/h** overnight water leak.
  - Switch the language toggle to **FR** to show French executive fluency, then return to **EN**.

### Step 2: The Executive Dashboard (The 30-Second Test)
- **URL**: `/dashboard`
- **Talking Points**:
  - Point directly to the hero KPI: **78 / 100 Resyntel Efficiency Score** against the Moroccan benchmark target of 88.
  - Point to the quantified savings: **65,200 MAD / year** across 17 verified operational opportunities.
  - Explain that the CEO does not need an engineering degree to see that **Energy is +12%** and **Water is +18%** above expected baseline.
  - Show the prominent red banner: An active **5.9 m³/hr** overnight water leak has been detected.

### Step 3: Energy Intelligence & The ONEE Model
- **URL**: `/energy`
- **Talking Points**:
  - Review the actual vs. expected monthly electricity chart.
  - Explain that Resyntel normalizes consumption against **Marrakech Cooling Degree Days (CDD)** and **PMS guest occupancy**.
  - Show that air conditioning (HVAC) accounts for 48% of the power bill.
  - Point out that chillers are drawing elevated power during ONEE’s peak tariff window (18:00–22:00 at 1.80 MAD/kWh).

### Step 4: Water Intelligence & The Overnight Leak
- **URL**: `/water`
- **Talking Points**:
  - This is the single highest-impact operational demo moment.
  - Point to the diurnal flow profile between **02:00 and 05:00 UTC**.
  - Expected nocturnal baseload is **1.8 m³/h**, but observed draw is **5.4–5.9 m³/h**.
  - Explain the diagnosis: A continuous leak in Guest Riser B is draining **4,500 m³ annually**, costing **18,700 MAD / year** at RADEEMA rates.

### Step 5: Carbon Footprint & Environmental Governance
- **URL**: `/carbon`
- **Talking Points**:
  - Demonstrates that sustainability is an automatic byproduct of resource efficiency.
  - Show Scope 1 (boilers/diesel) and Scope 2 (ONEE grid at **0.660 kg CO₂e / kWh**).
  - Annual addressable carbon reduction: **41.4 tCO₂e**.
  - Carbon intensity metric: **14.2 kg CO₂e / guest-night**.

### Step 6: Asset Intelligence & Mechanical Health
- **URL**: `/assets`
- **Talking Points**:
  - Show the health scoring matrix: Chillers (62%), Boilers (88%), Booster Pumps (71%).
  - Explain that Resyntel detects mechanical degradation (fouled heat exchangers, cavitation) *before* guests complain about hot water or lukewarm AC.

### Step 7: The Savings Opportunities Matrix
- **URL**: `/opportunities`
- **Talking Points**:
  - Sort by **Payback Period**.
  - Highlight the top two interventions:
    1. *Nocturnal Water Leak Isolation*: 2,500 MAD capex → **18,700 MAD / yr savings** → **1.6 months payback**.
    2. *Chiller Setpoint Optimization (+1.5°C)*: 6,000 MAD capex → **18,400 MAD / yr savings** → **3.9 months payback**.
  - Open the diagnostic drawer on the chiller item to show the step-by-step engineering work order.

### Step 8: Ask Resyntel (Deterministic AI Copilot)
- **URL**: `/ask`
- **Talking Points**:
  - Type or click: *“What should we fix first?”*
  - The AI responds deterministically: Three actions account for 72% of the savings, recommending the water leak and chiller setpoint first.
  - Emphasize to the CEO: *“This AI does not hallucinate. It is grounded in verified Moroccan utility math.”*

### Step 9: Portfolio Overview & Group Governance
- **URL**: `/portfolio`
- **Talking Points**:
  - Show how hotel group ownership compares multiple properties:
    - Zephyr Marrakech (180 keys, score 78, 65,200 MAD opportunity).
    - Zephyr Casablanca (210 keys, score 82, 84,100 MAD opportunity).
    - Zephyr Taghazout (150 keys, score 85, 49,300 MAD opportunity).
